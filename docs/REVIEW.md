## User Testing

I asked my sister to try the dashboard without explaining how it works.

- **Finding the training tasks:** She was able to find them easily.
- **Completing a task:** She understood how to complete a training task.
- **Understanding the percentage:** She understood what the progress percentage represented without needing an explanation.
- **Confusion:** She did not find anything confusing and did not need additional instructions.
- **Feedback:** She said everything was clear and easy to understand.
- **Improvement:** No changes were necessary based on this feedback.
